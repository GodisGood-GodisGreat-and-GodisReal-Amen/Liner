    Composition

    Balanced keeps the music and artwork in front: when several extras are on, the logo, captions, labels and visualizer step back a little, the visualizer softens while dancers are on screen, captions get a soft backing over busy areas, extras arrive a beat after the main picture, and new captions land in empty space. As set draws everything exactly as its sliders say.

    Background

    Flowing colour drawn from each song's artwork.

    Drop a videoMP4, MOV, WebM, GIF… It plays on a loop behind everything; its sound is ignored.

    Save mix

    Mixes save themselves as you work. This writes a copy of the mix together with its songs, covers, dancers, logo and videos, as a safeguard or to take it elsewhere.

    Load mix

    Saved copies in Liner’s Mixes folder. Loading one makes a new mix from it; any song or video that is no longer in the cache comes back from the copy.
